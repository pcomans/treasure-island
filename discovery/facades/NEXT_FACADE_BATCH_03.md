## Bounded execution rules

1. Work in table order, one target at a time. Resolve identity and dated official coverage before writing appearance claims.
2. Reconcile every defensible observed elevation to exact run endpoints before any live placement. Keep unknown, occluded, re-entrant, and back/courtyard chains explicit.
3. Treat building use, memorial/naval/fire/education names, common operator, proximity, and shared address as identity/context only. They do not establish style, material, signage, or module reuse.
4. Keep facade research separate from height/levels/default-massing discrepancies. This queue authorizes no runtime, geometry, source, collision, navigation, spray, terrain, foundation, or generated-data change.
5. Stop at a precise research blocker when identity or official public-elevation evidence is insufficient; do not create a prototype from a use label alone.
