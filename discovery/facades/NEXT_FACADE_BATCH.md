## Bounded execution order

1. Research exactly one target at a time in the table order; do not skip ahead merely because another facade appears easier.
2. First reconcile every observed public elevation to exact generated run endpoints and record blind/occluded sides. If that join cannot be defended, stop at a Museum-style research/spec blocker and do not integrate.
3. Separate facade-language findings from massing discrepancies. The queue authorizes no footprint, height, levels, roof, collision, navigation, terrain, or generated-data change.
4. Test reuse only after observation. Building name, use, date, proximity, or shared heritage metadata cannot establish a material or module family by itself.
5. Preserve absent addresses as absent, record observation dates, and never promote Street View imagery to evidence of current condition beyond its capture date.
