The review asks whether the diagnosed checker, quilting, banding, cellular, or blotch defect is gone without changing the declared scale, erasing the reference-supported material identity, inventing a motif or seam, or becoming unstable under changed light. Standalone material and receiver/live eligibility remain separate. No verdict authorizes attachment by itself.

Verdicts:

- `ACCEPT_WITH_LIMITATION`: the prescribed defect is removed and the corrected standalone field is useful within its target-specific evidence and scale limits.
- `REJECT_CORRECTABLE`: the single authorized correction did not satisfy its complete stated replacement brief. This review does not authorize a second correction attempt.
- `REJECT_REFERENCE_BLOCKED`: visual art may be reviewable, but the proof cannot be bound to the named target because its evidence identity/lineage record is invalid.
