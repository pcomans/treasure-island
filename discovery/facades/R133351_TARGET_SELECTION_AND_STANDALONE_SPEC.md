## Motif grammar and ownership

### Central/eastern light region

```text
LIGHT_FIELD + zero-or-one complete HWIN_LIGHT motif
```

- `LIGHT_FIELD` owns every nonopening wall pixel in the standalone study.
- `HWIN_LIGHT` owns one complete dark outer frame, one complete muted dark glazing face, and a bounded set of internal mullions. It owns no pale wall surround, pier, header strip, recess, shadow, collision, or opening cutout.
- Pane count is not evidence-backed. The factory may use one restrained symmetric count as an explicitly unsurveyed production inference, but it may not imply a facade sequence or create alternate pane-count assets.

### Western olive/lower region

```text
OLIVE_FIELD + zero-or-one complete WEST_LOWER motif
WEST_LOWER := full window frame + full dark face/mullions + one bounded LOWER panel
```

- `OLIVE_FIELD` owns every wall pixel outside the module.
- `WEST_LOWER` may own the discrete red-brown panel that is semantically part of the complete observed window/lower family. It owns no olive wall backing or fascia.
- `MAT-LOWER` is also shown independently so its color/roughness can be judged without treating the module as a repeating schedule.

No sequence, alternate, count, spacing, floor grid, entry rhythm, or cross-region transfer is encoded.

## Seam, boundary-product, and repeat contract

Legal seams:

- Within one uninterrupted same-ID homogeneous field when the same local metre-space phase is preserved.
- Within the bounded red-brown lower material only; it may not continue past its eventual proven local region.
- At the full outer frame/panel boundary of a complete module. Modules are placed as complete motifs and are not repeated by UV tiling.

Expected boundary products:

| Output | Left + right | Top + bottom | Four corners |
|---|---|---|---|
| Each homogeneous field | same field, no opening/pier/panel token created | same field, no band or story token created | same field; no cross, synchronized stain, or facade-scale mark |

- Any generated U reset used as architecture or cadence.
- Cuts through glazing, mullions, frames, discrete lower panel, piers/columns, doors, header/parapet/eave, recess depth, material transition, wing junction, corner, short facet, inner ring, charcoal-volume boundary, fence/tree occlusion, or sign.
- Any join that creates, widens, narrows, duplicates, or deletes a pane, frame member, panel, opening, or material region.
- Any transition from light to olive or red-brown based only on similar direction or neighboring run.
