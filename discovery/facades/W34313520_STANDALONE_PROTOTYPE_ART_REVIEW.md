## Why the target evidence is sufficient at this stage

This is absolute target-based acceptance, not a comparison with the shared gray placeholder. The September 2025 target-identifiable SSE observation directly establishes the only claims these assets make: a pale warm-gray/off-white smooth painted-concrete/render-like field with matte-to-low-satin behavior; dark gray-blue horizontal glazing/frame groups; and a broad three-tier stack separated by pale field/pier space on the 11 m target. It does not establish mullion cadence, exact bay period, dimensions, entries, lower/recess bounds, or other sides, and the prototypes correctly omit those claims.

`W34313520-MAT-PALE` matches the supported local material identity without baking openings, piers, joints, exposure, or directional lighting into the field. `W34313520-BAY` matches the supported three-tier semantic family as three complete, unsubdivided, fully framed horizontal groups. Its omission of invented mullions and its reliance on continuous host field for outer/inter-tier space make it more truthful to the available evidence than a detailed guessed facade tile would be. These observations are enough to accept bounded standalone candidates for calibration; they are not enough to accept their final receiver scale or composition.

## Asset-specific visual findings

### `W34313520-MAT-PALE`

- The neutral close frame reads warm off-white rather than cool concrete gray. Its extremely restrained variation is appropriate for the observed smooth painted/render-like field and does not manufacture blocks, panels, stains, or story bands.
- Scalar roughness and very shallow procedural relief avoid plastic gloss in neutral light. Under changed light, the field shifts from cream to a darker neutral gray-green response without a fixed exposure patch, baked sun direction, false metallic reflection, or inverted relief cue.
- Across the 30 m macro frame the field stays visually continuous and quiet. There is no visible repeating square, four-way cross, aligned speckle, synchronized weathering patch, or facade-scale focal pattern.
- `0.95 m` primary and `0.29 m` secondary procedural scales are acceptable proof assumptions inside the declared uncertainty, but they are not target measurements. Exact receiver UV phase, grazing response, and ordinary third-person scale still require calibration.

### `W34313520-BAY`

- The motif reads as one three-tier family: three wide horizontal dark groups, each with a complete four-sided frame, aligned on one vertical axis and separated by generous pale host-field space.
- The glazing-to-frame ratio is credible for the observed institutional SSE family. The dark blue-gray frame and muted teal-gray glazing remain distinguishable in neutral views; under changed light they compress toward dark values without producing a false mirror or metallic flare.
- The nominal width occupies less than one quarter of the 30 m proof wall and the height remains below the 11 m roof line with visible head/sill space. This is plausible enough for calibration but cannot settle real bay width, tier heights, pier spacing, or level count.
- Oblique evidence confirms shallow frame/glazing relief and continuous wall around every element. There is no hidden 6.8 × 9.4 m field slab, no rectangular color discontinuity, and no requirement that the complete BAY tile across a wall.
- One isolated BAY at ordinary distance remains readable without implying repetition. The absence of mullions, entry fragments, lower/recess elements, and the uncertain glazed wing is evidence discipline, not motif incompleteness.
