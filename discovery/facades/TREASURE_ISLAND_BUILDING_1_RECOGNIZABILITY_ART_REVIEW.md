## Recognizability conclusion

**Yes.** At the project's present stylization and gameplay scale, the after composition reads recognizably as the intended Administration Building 1 / Pan Am fair-building facade rather than as the same generic curved mass with a color change.

The three same-pose comparisons establish the improvement directly. The public-curve after view gains a legible sequence of dense tall multipane central openings with retained plain piers; the north-end after view gains a dominant grouped pavilion opening and subordinate base apertures; and the whole-building after view exposes a coherent family transition from small two-level wing windows through the denser center to distinct end groups. The warm-ivory field, dark blue-grey glazing, isolated blue service door, and restrained rose accents support the locally observed hierarchy without pretending to reproduce a surveyed schedule.

This is a **game-art recognizability acceptance**, not whole-building as-built acceptance. The invariant uniform `20 m` extrusion still cannot reproduce the observed lower wings, taller pavilions, stepped roof edge, or real entrance/canopy. Exact dimensions, anchors, count, cadence, sequence, and complete elevations remain unclaimed.
