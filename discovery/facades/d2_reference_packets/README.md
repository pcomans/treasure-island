## Authoritative corrections and lifecycle boundary

- The live San Francisco Enterprise Addressing System (EAS) has one exact
  record for every D2 address, all on parcel `1939001`. An active address point
  proves civic identity, not that its building survives or is occupied.
- The Navy's 2015 Site 12 table binds all 15 addresses and then-current housing
  providers. Its Figure 5 binds unit positions and garages. It contains no
  verified exterior elevation usable as facade evidence.
- Navy's 2020 review supports only the cohort grammar: planned early-1960s / 1965
  construction, two-storey slab-on-grade 4/6/8-unit housing, backyards, grass,
  paved roads and parking. It cannot be transferred as an exact opening schedule.
- TIDA's 2026 John Stewart Company amendment proves `1437 Chinook Court` and
  `1449 Croaker Court` were vacant buildings removed from those managed premises
  effective 2026-02-01. It does **not** prove demolition.
- `1436`, `1438`, `1439`, `1444`, `1418`, and `1420` appear on the agreement's
  2025 managed-building list/map. `1440`, `1442`, `1445`, and `1447` appear in
  TIDA's 2024 HR360 sublease through 2027, subject to early termination. These
  are lifecycle facts, not exterior observations.
- Provider/program groupings changed over time. They must not become invented
  signs or color schemes.

## Cohort grammar and recognition gate

The exact footprint family itself is useful: most units are similarly sized,
zigzagged row blocks with alternating long faces, recessed links and projecting
ends. The observed subset adds shallow dark-shingle gables, deep eaves, muted
stucco fields, paired rectangular windows, and recessed two-level balcony or
breezeway voids. That is a **shared cohort grammar**, never evidence for an
unseen target.

A D2 building can pass only through at least two coherent, target-bound cues at
a mapped side, while remaining distinguishable from the other 14 D2 buildings
and the adjacent Halibut/Sturgeon/Gateview rows. An address plaque, provider
name, color swatch, generic balcony, random damage, or exact footprint alone
does not pass. Tileable generated stucco/shingle fields are encouraged; an
imperfect seam is acceptable in a prototype, but low-frequency weathering and
opening/balcony hierarchy must prevent obvious wallpaper repetition.
