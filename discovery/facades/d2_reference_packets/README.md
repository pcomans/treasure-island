## Cohort grammar and recognition gate

The exact footprint family itself is useful: most units are similarly sized,
zigzagged row blocks with alternating long faces, recessed links and projecting
ends. The observed subset adds shallow dark-shingle gables, deep eaves, muted
stucco fields, paired rectangular windows, and recessed two-level balcony or
breezeway voids. That is a **shared cohort grammar**, never evidence for an
unseen target.

A D2 building can pass only through at least two coherent, target-bound cues at
a mapped side, while remaining distinguishable from the other 14 D2 buildings
and the adjacent Halibut/Sturgeon/Gateview rows. An address plaque, provider
name, color swatch, generic balcony, random damage, or exact footprint alone
does not pass. Tileable generated stucco/shingle fields are encouraged; an
imperfect seam is acceptable in a prototype, but low-frequency weathering and
opening/balcony hierarchy must prevent obvious wallpaper repetition.
