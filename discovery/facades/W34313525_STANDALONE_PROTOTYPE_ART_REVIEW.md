## Decision

**Aggregate verdict: `ACCEPT_WITH_DOCUMENTED_LIMITATION`.**

The sealed native package is sufficient for absolute standalone acceptance of one motif-free pale host field and four complete bounded opening motifs. The field reads as the observed warm pale, smooth matte service-wall family rather than as a decorated wall; the modules preserve the observed roll-up, personnel-door, and high-group hierarchy without taking ownership of surrounding field. Their restrained geometry and material response remain credible under neutral and changed light at close and ordinary proof distances.

The limitation is substantive but belongs to the next lifecycle stage: color, finish scale, dimensions, placement, count, cadence, and sequence are not surveyed. `W34313525-HIGH-GROUP` also remains deliberately unresolved as window versus grille. Acceptance here therefore authorizes detached exact-receiver calibration only within the explicit evidence-region bounds below. It does not authorize direct attachment or treat the combined lineup as a real facade layout.

No asset correction is required. The evidence shows complete motifs, plausible standalone proportions on a 5 m comparison wall, stable material families, shallow depth, zero module-owned backing, and readable close/ordinary behavior. Remaining uncertainty cannot be made more truthful by changing the standalone art without receiver calibration or stronger reference evidence.

## Independent visual findings

### `W34313525-MAT-PALE`

- The broad center/right span in `01-neutral-field-close.png` is a warm off-white/light-gray matte field with very low-contrast, nonsemantic variation. It contains no roll-up slats, door shapes, high-window cadence, broad repair camouflage, directional highlight, cast-shadow repair, or other baked architectural/light motif.
- The slight clipped edge of `HIGH-GROUP` at the far left of image `01` is an evidence-framing artifact, not part of the material claim. It does not obstruct the large clear material sample, and the dedicated high-group, ordinary, and changed-light views independently cover that module.
- Across the full `30 m` span in `07-macro-repeat-span.png`, the field remains visually subordinate: no panel boundary, phase reset, repeated repair blob, exposure band, grid, or conspicuous macro echo emerges. Because this is a continuous aperiodic shader rather than a finite bitmap cell, numeric opposite-edge identity is correctly `N/A`.
- Under the changed sun in image `08`, the field moves toward a cooler/darker gray while retaining a diffuse matte read. That response is consistent with neutral albedo plus subtle procedural relief rather than baked illumination. The relief is intentionally quiet; its exact physical frequency remains a calibration question.
- The observed target supports the pale smooth matte family and excludes global repair repetition. This is positive target evidence, not merely improvement over the placeholder. The prototype does not claim an exact substrate among masonry, render, or panel-like construction.

### `W34313525-ROLLUP-PALE`

- Image `02` shows one complete opening: the pale leaf, all four outer frame bars, and all eight horizontal slat bars are visible. No edge cuts the leaf, frame, or slat grammar.
- The roughly square industrial-door proportion is plausible against the 5 m proof wall. The pale leaf remains distinct from both the warmer host field and dark frame at close and ordinary distance.
- In the changed-light oblique view, the shallow leaf/frame/slat offsets produce restrained edge shadows without a field-colored slab, halo, or surrounding patch. The module owns no wall field and is not being judged as a repeat cell.

### `W34313525-ROLLUP-GRAY`

- Image `03` likewise contains a complete gray leaf, full four-sided frame, and eight-bar slat field. Neighbor fragments do not obscure or redefine its boundary.
- The gray state is materially distinct from the pale roll-up while retaining the same complete opening grammar, consistent with the reference observation of visibly different roll-up states. The difference remains legible in images `06` and `08`.
- Proportion and depth remain plausible at standalone scale, with no backing panel or fabricated surrounding repair region. Nothing in this acceptance says the two roll-up states alternate or share a cadence.

### `W34313525-PERSONNEL`

- Image `04` shows a complete plain vertical leaf and all four frame edges. Its restrained detail is compatible with the observed plain personnel/service-door family; adding unsupported signage, glazing, hardware, or utility clutter would be less truthful.
- The human-door aspect ratio reads clearly beside the roll-ups in image `06`, providing a useful relative scale cue without claiming measured dimensions. The shallow frame separates the motif from the host wall without taking ownership of the surround.
- Neutral and changed-light views preserve a matte gray/pale leaf and dark frame with no metallic or glass-like misread.

### `W34313525-HIGH-GROUP`

- The corrected straight-on image `05` shows the entire full outer frame, one dark opaque face, and both internal mullions; three complete horizontal subdivisions remain legible. The motif is neither clipped nor dependent on the image-01 edge fragment.
- Its low, wide proportion and elevated placement on the 5 m comparison wall are compatible with the supported NNW high-window/grille family. It remains readable in the ordinary combined view and as shallow projection under changed light.
- The opaque face appropriately refuses to choose between glazing and grille semantics that the source does not resolve. Acceptance covers the bounded dark high group only; it does not promote glazing properties, transparency, grille pitch, or cross-side use.

### Combined standalone read

- Images `06` and `08` preserve a coherent one-storey service-facade vocabulary: two large roll-up states, one narrower personnel door, and one elevated horizontal group remain distinguishable against a continuous pale host field.
- Every module reads as a shallow attachment with a complete semantic boundary. The `38` module meshes contain zero field/backing meshes, and no visible patch edge, slab halo, or false field reset contradicts that ownership contract.
- The lineup is a comparison board, not a proposed elevation. Its visual spacing cannot establish side order, count, alternation, cadence, or along-run coordinates. The roll-ups are supported for the SSE family, the high group for the NNW family, and the personnel door for both recorded families; displaying them together does not erase those bounds.
