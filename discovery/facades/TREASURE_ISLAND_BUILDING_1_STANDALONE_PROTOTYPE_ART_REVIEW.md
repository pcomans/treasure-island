## Asset findings

### `B1-MAT-IVORY`

The neutral close view reads as a smooth warm-ivory painted-mineral/stucco-like field rather than masonry, metal, wood, or a photographic wall. Its low-amplitude variation remains non-focal, with no baked opening, band, ground line, roof line, directional runoff streak, highlight, shadow, or unique repair mark. The separate rose band low in image `01` is visibly geometry-owned and is not part of the material field.

Across the full `40 m` image `02`, the procedural field stays quiet without a visible grid, synchronized blotch, exposure stripe, per-panel restart, or facade-scale period. Image `11` shifts the field darker and cooler under the changed sun while retaining a diffuse matte response; it does not reveal metallic glare, fixed illumination, or inverted relief. The declared `1.10 / 0.32 m` scales and `0.82` roughness are visually plausible but unsurveyed.

### `B1-WING-W`

Image `03` shows one intact shorter, wider punched opening with an opaque blue-grey face, full four-sided pale surround, one vertical mullion, and one horizontal mullion. No piece is clipped, stretched into the central family, or duplicated at its boundary. Its square-ish `1.70 x 1.70 m` nominal study remains inside the observation-derived uncertainty bracket and reads plausibly beside the door and taller families. The far-right pavilion sliver is a framing artifact, not part of this motif.

### `B1-CENTRAL-W`

Image `04` shows one complete tall narrow opening with full surround, one vertical mullion, and three horizontal mullions producing a coherent four-row study. Its proportions remain clearly distinct from `B1-WING-W`, matching the observed shift to denser tall multipane openings without claiming the real curved-center sequence. Neighboring motifs at the frame edges are contextual only.

### `B1-PAV-W`

Image `05` shows the complete dominant pavilion group used by this asset: full outer frame, three-column/five-row opaque multipane face, and two full-height shallow side strips. The module remains uncropped and materially coherent, and its `5.40 x 10.00 m` nominal size stays within the reference-derived `4..7 x 8..14 m` bracket. It reads as the dominant group rather than a stretched wing or central window. This does not accept flanking-opening placement, a pavilion schedule, stepped roof-edge geometry, emblems, or the real taller-pavilion silhouette.

### `B1-BASE-O`

Image `06` shows a complete small base opening with full four-sided surround and center divider. The opaque dark face correctly avoids fabricating transparency, an interior, louver detail, or a definitive window/vent function. Scale is plausible relative to the door but remains unmeasured.

### `B1-DOOR-BLUE`

Image `07` shows a complete dark-blue opaque leaf and full pale frame with a credible personnel/utility-door proportion. The simple leaf is consistent with the limited observation and does not invent glazing, hardware, signage, access state, or main-entrance/canopy semantics. The neighboring base-opening fragment is outside the named motif.

### `B1-BAND`

Image `08` exposes the entire `4.20 x 0.38 m` finite rose base-band exemplar, both explicit terminals, and its shallow depth. The restrained low-chroma rose is consistent with the observed parking-wing base accent and stays distinct from the warm-ivory field. Its finite terminals make it a complete exemplar rather than an edge-cut texture; they do not establish the target's real termination shape, length, continuity, or permission to use the same rose treatment for lighter belt/cornice bands.

## Detached calibration eligibility

All seven standalone assets pass their independent art gate and may be carried forward as candidate inputs. They are **not yet eligible to be placed on a detached exact receiver** from the current evidence alone.

A later calibration round must first supply exact endpoint-mapped observed outer runs with explicit `outer` ring ownership. It may then test:

- `B1-MAT-IVORY` for scale, continuous metre-space phase, and ordinary/changed-light behavior only over the proven material scope;
- `B1-WING-W`, `B1-CENTRAL-W`, and `B1-PAV-W` only on their corresponding mapped public-outer semantic regions;
- `B1-BASE-O`, `B1-DOOR-BLUE`, and `B1-BAND` only on a mapped observed base region; and
- one or a deliberately sparse bounded set of exemplars without using the proof lineup as a count, order, interval, alternation, or complete side schedule.

Calibration must preserve full motif geometry, host-field ownership, zero backing, maximum nominal relief `0.12 m`, and receiver collision/spray/massing invariants. Cardinal facing buckets alone are insufficient because outer and inner runs can share similar normals.
