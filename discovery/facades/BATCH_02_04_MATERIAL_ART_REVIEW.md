## Decision boundary

This is an absolute review of the declared `homogeneous_material_tile` candidates, not a comparison with the current gray placeholder walls. A homogeneous field is not penalized for omitting windows, doors, signs, bands, or other module/elevation content. It is rejected when its material-scale pattern invents a facade-scale read, reads as the wrong material, has an unstable or conspicuous repeat, aliases, or uses an unsupported scale.

All proofs are `4.8 × 3.2 m` controlled flat panels rendered in Forward+/Metal at `1440 × 900`, with one close, one ordinary-distance, and one changed-light oblique view. They are not gameplay captures, exact receiver proof, seam-on-building proof, or whole-object acceptance. Every registry declares its physical scale as a reversible, unmeasured proof assumption. Consequently no candidate receives an unrestricted `ACCEPT`; every passing standalone material retains a scale/substrate and exact-receiver limitation.

Verdicts mean:

- `ACCEPT_WITH_LIMITATION`: suitable as the declared standalone material field within the stated observation/temporal scope; the stated limitation remains binding.
- `REJECT_CORRECTABLE`: standalone material fails one visible art gate; the row and correction brief prescribe one bounded correction.
- `REJECT_REFERENCE_BLOCKED`: the source evidence cannot support a standalone material judgment. None of the 26 registry entries needs this verdict because each has at least a bounded material/color brief; several remain reference- or mapping-blocked for live use.
- Receiver/live eligibility is a separate column. “Integration-trial candidate” authorizes only a later bounded implementation and receiver capture on the exact listed runs. It is not attachment acceptance.

Evidence shorthand in the tables: `M` is the exact capture manifest; `C`, `O`, and `L` are the close, ordinary-distance, and changed-light-oblique PNGs; `S` is the target reference spec. The observation ledgers and exact run maps are [Batch 02 observations](BATCH_02_STREETVIEW_OBSERVATIONS.md), [Batch 03 observations](BATCH_03_STREETVIEW_OBSERVATIONS.md), [Batch 04 observations](BATCH_04_STREETVIEW_OBSERVATIONS.md), [Batch 02 run map](BATCH_02_RUN_MAPPING.md), [Batch 03 run map](BATCH_03_RUN_MAPPING.md), and [Batch 04 run map](BATCH_04_RUN_MAPPING.md).
