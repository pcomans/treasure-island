- **WINSTACK:** the narrow white outer frame and two horizontal rails yield three complete dark panes. Its tall proportion remains subordinate to the full 6 m wall and retains useful sill/head clearance; it does not become a painted slit or facade-height panel.

- **SERVICE:** the pale gray-green leaf and seven shallow horizontal seams read as one service opening, not host siding copied over a rectangle. Its complete frame clears the roof and meets the sloped ground without a visible buried bottom bar.
- **Contact and depth:** the helper's shallow `0.06 m` wall offset and maximum `0.18 m` front extent produce readable frame shadow without an air gap, projecting envelope, or collision-scale relief.
- **Ordinary gameplay:** WINSTACK and ENTRY remain clearly distinct across the long SSE blank span; SERVICE remains readable alone on ENE. This is good calibration rhythm precisely because it does not pretend to reproduce an every-bay sequence.
- **Two-side contribution:** the combined frame establishes a coherent cool-siding/opening family across the two supported regions. The intervening protected placeholder facets make the evidence boundary visible and prevent an accidental claim of continuous accepted treatment around the building.
- **Changed light:** SSE trim, glass/entry, and siding retain their material/value hierarchy with no baked highlight, false metallic flare, or inverted relief. Exact ENE SERVICE response under that light remains unreviewed.
