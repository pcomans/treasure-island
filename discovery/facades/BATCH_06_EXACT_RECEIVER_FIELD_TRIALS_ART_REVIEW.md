## Final decision

**All four exact-receiver homogeneous-field scopes: ACCEPT_WITH_LIMITATION.**

Each field is assigned only to the observed material role and side chain supported by the final Batch 06 mapping. The three smooth fields remain nonsemantic and subordinate: they introduce no facade-scale panel, belt, opening, stain grid, or directional baked light. The siding field preserves a plausible `0.20 m` horizontal course read without shimmer or phase reset. Wall-tangent horizontal metres and world-vertical metres produce comparable feature scale across the tested wall orientations. Corners and accepted-to-placeholder boundaries are geometrically closed, terrain contact remains grounded, and changed light preserves matte/low-satin behavior.

The physical finish/grain/course scales remain unmeasured reversible assumptions. Acceptance applies to the field behavior on the exact mapped run surfaces, not to every vertical pixel as a final facade assignment. In particular, it does not accept the unresolved `w34313564` plinth extent, `w34313515` lower/recess region, `w291196370` trim/accent/opening regions, any module, or any unobserved side.

All four fields are **eligible to keep in the current controlled trial** on the exact runs below; no art-based revert is required. They are only **eligible for a separate lifecycle-promotion decision**. This review does not itself promote or authorize permanent live attachment.

No asset, shader, material, helper, runtime, test, generated file, lifecycle inventory, capture, or evidence artifact was changed by this review.

## Per-building findings

### `w34313564` — tan SSE and pale ENE/NNW

**Reference/material identity:** The tan field is confined to mapped SSE runs `5..9`; the pale field is confined to mapped ENE `10..13` and NNW `14..19`. This preserves the observed side-specific tan/pale split instead of normalizing it as light. The changed-light `9→10` corner retains an unequivocal material-family change at the actual SSE/ENE corner without color bleed or an extra facade panel.

**Physical scale and repetition:** The `0.82 m` tan and `0.88 m` pale aperiodic fields read as fine, subordinate finish variation rather than architectural marks. Their precise real-world finish scale is not measurable from the references, but neither looks grossly oversized at the close view or turns into a grid, synchronized blotch, exposure band, pixelation, or alias at ordinary distance. Both remain mostly quiet, which is appropriate for the declared homogeneous-field role.

**Orientation and corners:** The pale `13→14` ENE/NNW corner shows comparable variation size on both orientations. Normal-driven lighting changes value across the corner, but no horizontal stretch, vertical scale drift, repeated column, crack, or torn triangle appears. Aperiodic phase continuity is not required across a physical corner because no semantic motif crosses it.

**Boundary and terrain contact:** The `4→5` placeholder-to-tan boundary is closed and coplanar with no overlap, gap, Z-fight, or geometry tear. It is nevertheless a conspicuous artificial material cut on one collinear SSE source edge. That is acceptable only as an explicitly controlled evidence boundary; it is not accepted as a real architectural seam and prevents this review from implying whole-side or whole-building finish truth. All shown wall bottoms meet the terrain; the dark perimeter strip reads as contact/cast shadow, not a visible air gap.

**Changed light and ordinary play:** Tan and pale remain dielectric/matte under the changed sun. Pale reads consistently at the `26 m` ordinary view, and the side-family distinction remains legible without becoming the focal pattern. Missing plinth, windows, doors, pipes, eave/access geometry, and WSW treatment are outside these field verdicts.

### `w34313515` — pale WSW/NNW field

**Reference/material identity:** The field is confined to the two mapped outer chains actually observed in September 2025: WSW `0..8` and NNW `43..47`. Its pale warm-gray/off-white, smooth matte-to-low-satin hierarchy matches the reference role and does not invent a belt, podium, opening, window rhythm, or story division.

**Physical scale, orientation, and repetition:** The `1.10 m` aperiodic grain remains a reversible unmeasured assumption, but the close view shows no facade-scale blotch or false relief and the ordinary NNW view collapses to a stable quiet field rather than a grid. The accepted wrap corner `47→0` keeps comparable feature size across NNW and WSW; the orientation-dependent value difference reads as ordinary lighting. No repeated band, alias, metallic highlight, or texture stretch appears.

**Boundary, terrain contact, and changed light:** The `8→9` accepted-to-placeholder transition is a clean geometric corner with no bleed, overlap, crack, or false coplanar patch. The wall remains grounded along the sloped/paved terrain in ordinary, corner, and changed-light views; no floating lower edge is visible. Changed light preserves a neutral smooth field without baked highlights or inverted relief.

**Ordinary play limitation:** The field remains readable as a material family at `28 m`, but it intentionally supplies no bays, recesses, lower-value region, or facade cadence. Full-height pale coverage in this trial does not resolve the observed darker lower/recess family, whose vertical and horizontal ownership remain reference-blocked.

### `w291196370` — horizontal siding SSE/ENE field

**Reference/material identity:** The cool gray-blue siding is restricted to observed SSE/public runs `8..10` and ENE/end runs `17..22`. It does not propagate to NNW, WSW, service facets, or yard-hidden chains, and it does not invent dividers, openings, signs, or access geometry.

**Physical scale and aliasing:** Roughly thirty `0.20 m` courses span the `6 m` receiver height, a plausible provisional siding read. Course height is still unmeasured against the official reference. Lines are clean and subordinate in the close view, remain distinguishable without moiré/shimmer at the `27 m` ordinary view, and retain stable contrast under changed light. Broad color variation does not create vertical panels or an accidental larger repeat.

**Orientation and phase:** Separate SSE and ENE captures show the same world-vertical course density, and the exact material uses world `VERTEX.y` for course phase rather than generated U. The evidence contains no adjacent accepted SSE→ENE corner because protected runs `11..16` lie between those chains; therefore this acceptance does not claim continuity through those protected facets. It does establish orientation-invariant scale on the two tested accepted orientations.

**Boundaries and terrain contact:** The ENE `22→23` and SSE `10→11` accepted-to-placeholder corners end the siding cleanly with no doubled course, smear, gap, coplanar overlap, or geometry tear. Siding reaches the terrain without a visible floating edge; perimeter darkness reads as cast/contact shadow. Those placeholder boundaries remain evidence limits, not proposed architectural trim.

**Ordinary play limitation:** The siding family reads clearly at gameplay distance and remains matte/low-satin. Missing white dividers, parapet trim, window/entry/service modules, red-brown accents, and yard/access details are not field failures and are not accepted here.
