## Key visual findings

1. Actual-world field attachment is visually indistinguishable from the accepted detached proposal in the intended way: all five run joins remain invisible, and neither exact scope breaks into material patches.
2. The long SSE ordinary frame is an effective cadence safeguard. The two roll-ups are separated by a large blank field and retain different leaf states; they do not form an alternating or evenly repeated schedule.
3. The full NNW frame keeps the personnel door and high group visually distinct and subordinate. Their unlike roles prevent the pair from reading as a repeated unit.
4. All four close frames retain complete boundaries and natural shallow shadows, with host pale field visible around each motif. No field-colored backing, halo, duplicate receiver face, or z-fight appears.
5. Nominal ground contact remains credible for both roll-ups and the personnel door. This does not establish their maximum unmeasured height envelopes at the same centers.
6. Changed light preserves material hierarchy and exposes no baked illumination, metallic response, inverted relief, or false glazing claim.
7. Placeholder regions remain visibly different outside the bounded scopes. That incompleteness is truthful and prevents the live work from masquerading as a completed elevation or building.
8. The whole-island frame is load evidence only. The target is far below useful art-review scale, so it cannot promote whole-building resemblance.
