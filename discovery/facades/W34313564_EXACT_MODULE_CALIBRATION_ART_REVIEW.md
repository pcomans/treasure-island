The calibration registry's new face-level `module_roles_observed` assertion cannot silently broaden that established contract.

Even the four accepted calibrations are not eligible for live attachment. Their center coordinates, five-opening count, and layout are explicitly production inferences, while the preceding independent module review required reference-backed coordinates before attachment. A detached visual calibration can prove plausible scale, host compatibility, orientation, depth, contact, and readability; it cannot turn an unobserved coordinate or illustrative exemplar count into a truthful live facade. The current accepted tan/pale field lifecycle remains unchanged.

## Aggregate art findings

### Motif scale, proportions, and wall ownership

The HWIN opening is `4.25 m` wide and `1.20 m` high—about one quarter of the `5 m` wall height—so it reads as the observed broad upper horizontal multi-pane family, not a storefront band or story-scale aperture. Four complete panes remain visible at close range and collapse into a single dark horizontal opening family, rather than aliasing, at ordinary distance.

The PDOOR uses a conventional human-readable proportion. Its terrain-interpolated base plus `0.08 m` reversible clearance keeps the leaf and lower frame at the foundation. The close NNW and ordinary SSE/NNW views show contact or a narrow cast/contact shadow, not floating or deep terrain intersection. Neither module owns wall-colored pixels, plinth, collision, navigation, spray, stairs, ramps, rails, or landings.

### Orientation, depth, materials, and light

The five modules follow local X along the run tangent, local Y along world up, and local Z along the outward normal. The close SSE HWIN, oblique ENE HWIN, and ENE→NNW context show no billboard rotation, mirrored pane order, back-face loss, or depth inversion. Maximum shallow relief stays within `0.18 m`; frames and leaves cast enough local shadow to read without becoming projecting facade slabs.

The common dark-brown/gray frame and opaque dark glass work on both accepted host families. The gray door is clearly distinct from tan and intentionally subtler on pale, where its dark frame preserves the opening. Changed light keeps the ENE window dielectric and coherent; there is no fixed baked highlight or metallic flare.

### Corners, host transition, and ordinary gameplay

The `13→14` pale ENE→NNW corner stays geometrically closed and shows the modules correctly assigned to their respective outward planes. The `9→10` tan SSE→pale ENE boundary preserves the accepted material-family transition; the nearest windows remain inside their side/run scopes and do not bridge, clip, or create a false corner panel. Both ordinary views use a roughly player-height, `50°` camera and keep the door and window silhouettes distinguishable across the long elevations.

These context views establish layout readability, not real opening count or sequence. The broad blank intervals are acceptable in a calibration because the source explicitly says openings are irregular and no periodic cadence is proven.
