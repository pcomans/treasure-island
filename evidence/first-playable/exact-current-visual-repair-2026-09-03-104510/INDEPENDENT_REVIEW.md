## Jetpack: pixel presentation versus mechanical proof

Frames `11` and `12` are a credible combined rise-then-descent proof, with an important limitation:

- **Pixels:** both show the player airborne. In `12`, the avatar-to-shadow/ground separation is visibly smaller than in `11`, so the ordered pair communicates a lower altitude. Because the third-person camera follows the player and keeps the capsule nearly screen-locked, the images alone cannot prove input, velocity, or rule out an arbitrary camera/transform change.
- **Manifest:** public action `jetpack` / physical `Space` is pressed for `11` and released for `12` in one time-connected sequence. Frame `11` rises from `y=3.456963` to `y=6.291087` (`+2.834124 m`) at `+5.000 m/s` over 46 physics frames. Frame `12` is `y=5.222747`, `1.068339 m` below frame `11`, after a `1.950007 m` drop from the capped-descent start, at `-1.500 m/s`; it records 76 capped-descent frames, zero post-input transform writes, and zero recovery delta.

That combination merits **PASS_WITH_LIMITATION**, not an animation/VFX or human-input acceptance claim.

## Ranked remaining discrepancies

### Blocking final acceptance, but not entry into full verification

1. **Ordinary exact-package play is not shown here.** Walk, run, mouse camera, landing/recovery, rejected spray plus resumed movement, and pause/resume still require the package verification gate.
2. **Owner/local recognition is not shown.** This reviewer can judge the ensemble improved and coherent, but only the intended observer can close Treasure Island recognition acceptance.

### Nonblocking major quality limitations

1. The pale-cyan overlay boundary in `10` reads as an artificial slab/channel edge rather than finished shoreline treatment.
2. North/center/southwest/east building massing remains sparse and repetitive. External Bay context does considerable recognition work, especially in `02`–`05`, `07`, and `08`.
3. Jetpack state has no visual language beyond altitude and shadow separation; the fixed follow camera makes motion subtle without the ordered pair and metrics.

### Nonblocking minor limitations

1. The saturated cyan capsule is an allowed placeholder, but it dominates framing and does not change pose across walk/jetpack states.
2. The wall tag is readable but small; a closer oblique view would better expose edge containment.
3. Bridge/SF context is washed out against the flat gray sky, and several road/path joins remain angular.
