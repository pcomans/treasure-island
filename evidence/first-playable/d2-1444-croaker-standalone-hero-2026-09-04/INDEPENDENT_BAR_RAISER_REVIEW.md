### Distinction from 1441 and other Site-12 rows

The reviewed 1441 prototype is balanced around **two projecting, densely
windowed wings and a central paired two-level recess**. The reviewed 1444 is
instead strongly one-sided: **blank upper mass over two broad ground voids,
sparse middle openings, quiet pauses, and one narrow far-end stack**. That
difference remains obvious in grayscale and in silhouette/value rhythm; it is
not a palette distinction.

The 1439 packet describes one end balcony stack, a broad quiet upper field, and
a blank/passage end, so it is the closest known compositional risk. 1444 still
separates through its dominant two-field undercroft directly below the blank
upper block and its specific sparse-return progression. The frozen confusion
targets 1431 Halibut, 1436 Chinook, and 1449 Croaker do not have sufficient
target-side appearance evidence or reviewed implementations to prove their
unseen real facades differ. This review therefore does not manufacture a
source-level top-1 claim against unseen peers. Within the available truthful
game-art evidence, 1444 has a distinct target-bound composition; unknown peers
remain uncredited and must be tested in live context when evidence exists.

## GenAI stucco, tileability, and motion

The exact text-only GenAI warm-ivory albedo is reused byte-for-byte under a
scalar ochre tint at an assumed `3 m` repeat. The raw `2 x 2` inspection shows
faint low-frequency source-cell recurrence, but no hard vertical/horizontal
edge, object-shaped stain, directional highlight, shadow, vignette, or obvious
baked illumination. There is no generated normal, roughness, AO, height, or
displacement map that could contradict it.

In the six native stills and all `121` reviewed motion frames:

- no obvious seam or per-run phase reset appears;
- no shimmer, moire fan, crawling grid, popping motif, or distracting repeat
  develops as the stock player moves;
- changed light moves form shadows while the material remains neutral, so the
  albedo does not read as carrying a fixed light direction; and
- the texture mips into a quiet micro-surface at the widest distances. It adds
  modest material richness without attempting to carry building identity.

The source cell is discoverable under deliberate raw-repeat inspection and the
render can look almost scalar at distance. Both are nonblocking under the
project's progress-over-perfect tileability rule. No texture regeneration is
justified by this gate.

## Ranked nonblocking limitations

1. **Undercroft semantic ambiguity.** The two nearly black shallow fields can
   first read as large garage doors rather than a walk-through undercroft. In
   the live study, preserve the blank-over-void pair and test whether modest
   side/reveal or ground-through depth can improve the read without inventing
   an accessible interior. Do not replace the defining voids with windows.
2. **Far balcony legibility.** Thin rails merge into the dark recess in the
   whole view. If live context erases the stack, adjust only rail
   thickness/value or slab/recess separation; do not enlarge it into a 1441-like
   central balcony system.
3. **Economical eave/roof read.** The eave is a straight dark band and the
   shallow pitch is subtle. Verify live attachment does not flatten, double, or
   z-fight it; no spectacle-driven roof-rise increase is justified.
4. **Protected-side provisional finish.** The neutral module-free faces are
   visibly plain and the ochre/neutral boundary can be abrupt. That is an honest
   source boundary. Add no unsupported openings or copied cohort schedule.
5. **Faint stucco recurrence.** The raw cell fingerprint can be found when
   intentionally repeated, but it never becomes a distracting rendered grid or
   motion artifact. Keep the reviewed scale and phase for the first live study.
6. **No literal entrance/interior.** Human-scale access is implied by
   undercroft and balcony geometry rather than a door. This restraint is
   preferable to inventing an unsupported focal entry.
