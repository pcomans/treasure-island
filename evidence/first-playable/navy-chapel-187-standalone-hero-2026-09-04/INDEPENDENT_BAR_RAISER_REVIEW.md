## Decision

**PASS_WITH_LIMITATION for standalone promotion to one reversible live
integration.**

This is not denominator credit and does not accept a live Navy Chapel. It means
the standalone hero has crossed the recognition and believability threshold
needed to justify a fresh exact-receiver integration/evidence pass.

The after state is top-1 Navy Chapel from both ordinary grounded views without
its name, address, map location, source ID, or cream hue. It exposes the two
mandatory source-supported cues as complete architectural compositions:

1. the square belfry, pyramidal cap, and restrained cross create an unmistakable
   roof silhouette; and
2. the SSE gable, tall divided window, and shallow projecting entry read as one
   focal public-front hierarchy.

Three bounded tall narrow side-opening groups add a useful secondary rhythm.
The remaining sides stay quiet rather than receiving invented chapel wallpaper.

## Pixel-first before/after judgment

I opened the four original `1440x900` PNGs at original detail and fixed the
visual judgment before reading the README, manifest, labels, config, technical
results, source packet, or confusion packets.

### Before

Both exact-pose before frames show a low pale institutional block with a broad
blank end and a generic repeated side treatment. It has no focal public entry,
no chapel roof silhouette, and no structural opening hierarchy. It is not
recognizable as a chapel.

### After

At exactly the same poses, the object acquires:

- a strong gable front and dark pitched roof;
- a square tower rising clearly through the roof, terminated by a pyramidal cap
  and skyline-readable cross;
- one tall, deeply valued divided window centered above a genuinely projecting
  entry volume; and
- a small, controlled run of complete tall narrow side windows.

The improvement is not cosmetic. The after silhouette rises to `0.285` and
`0.234` of frame height versus `0.142` and `0.115` before, while the horizontal
footprint and camera poses remain unchanged. Roof, entry, and opening depth
remain visible at both approximately `60.7 m` and `72.9 m` LOS distances.

The entry is subordinate to the tall window but still legible as the base of
the same vertical composition. The side groups repeat just enough to imply a
nave/public-side rhythm; they do not spread over unknown elevations. Dark roof
and opening values, warm/light walls, and pale trim form a coherent low-poly
material hierarchy without surface noise or baked facade imagery.

## Source and confusion-set comparison

The P3 packet's strongest observations are all respected:

- **Gabled public entrance end:** present as one gable, one tall vertically and
  horizontally divided dark window, and one shallow projecting entry. Exact
  dimensions and pane divisions remain production inference rather than survey
  claims.
- **Belfry silhouette:** present as one square tower, one pyramidal cap, and one
  restrained cross. The cross supports the silhouette but is not being used as
  a label in place of architecture.
- **Partial long-side rhythm:** present only on adjacent mapped runs `11..13` as
  three complete tall narrow groups. The observed fact of a partial rhythm is
  source-supported; the count and anchors are explicitly inferred and
  reversible.
- **Warm light field and pale trim:** used as supporting values. Accepted cream
  remains isolated to exact SSE runs `9,10`; inferred new structure has a
  separate unaccepted material.

The packet also mentions small rectangular windows in part of the gable field.
Their omission is acceptable at this stage: the two strongest cue families are
complete, and adding uncertain small openings would not materially improve the
ordinary-player read.

Frozen decoys do not survive a top-1 comparison:

- Oasis `w291189918` is only a low warm mass with a shallow dark edge and broad
  central opening-band observation; it has no supported gable/belfry public
  composition.
- Dormitory 369 `w291189926` is a long five-tier facade family with a blue-gray
  stair/end volume and green eave/window hierarchy, not a chapel silhouette.
- Food Service 368 `w34313521` has no source-supported facade cues at all and
  therefore cannot match this complete roof/front composition.
- Other low California Avenue institutional blocks lack the combined belfry,
  cap, cross, gable, tall window, and projecting entry.

The result remains Navy Chapel in grayscale. Cream is support, not proof.

## Believability and smallest residual corrections

### Keep

- Keep the gable/roof/belfry/cap proportions and the single vertical
  window-to-entry axis. These are doing nearly all of the recognition work.
- Keep the real entry projection, deep opening values, pale complete frames,
  and collision-backed roof silhouette.
- Keep the side rhythm bounded to three groups on runs `11..13`; do not extend
  it around the building or present the count as observed.
- Keep protected runs and rear/private faces module-free.

### Change or verify, in priority order

1. **Give the entry one stronger contact read in the live version.** A small
   threshold/base and slightly clearer recess or shaded underside would make
   the doorway feel usable without adding columns, arches, signage, or an
   invented door program.
2. **Unify the quiet rear/return mass without inventing its facade.** Carry only
   physically necessary roof-edge/base continuity and tune the protected
   neutral value so the long continuation feels like the same maintained
   building rather than a diagnostic block. Add no windows or ornament.
3. **Test the thin elements in motion and changed light.** The cross and narrow
   mullions are readable in stills but near their minimum stable screen size.
   Thicken them only if a stock-player walk/jetpack sweep shows shimmer or
   disappearance; do not enlarge them for spectacle.
4. **Leave small gable-field openings deferred.** Add them only if a bounded
   placement can be supported and they materially improve the live read.

These are non-identity limitations. None blocks the first reversible live pass.
