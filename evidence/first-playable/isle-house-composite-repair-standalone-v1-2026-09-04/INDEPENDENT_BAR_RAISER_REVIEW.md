## Ranked blocking discrepancies

1. **The protected NNW return exposes a distracting repeat grid.** In the WSW
   oblique B frame, the return occupies a large, unobstructed portion of the
   low mass and retains the underlying generic/checkered material. This is not
   faint recurrence: it is the strongest surface pattern in that whole view.
   Because the proposed swap hides the failed live child and the repair authors
   no geometry on runs `10..12`, this appearance is representative of the
   proposed live result, not an A/B capture accident. Preserve the no-schedule
   boundary, but give this unknown return a quiet non-recurring homogeneous
   fallback or explicitly narrow the promotion so it cannot expose the grid.

2. **The public upper podium field is too blank to be believable.** Variant B
   correctly avoids inventing windows or a story count, but the large pale band
   has no material-scale variation, structural cadence, or sufficiently legible
   transition depth. It visually merges with the already quiet tower and reads
   as unfinished masking. The next attempt should add only source-safe mass,
   material, or shallow-depth hierarchy; it must not invent an upper window
   schedule or claim a literal seven-story reconstruction.

3. **The module variants do not separate strongly enough in play.** The four
   contracts are mechanically different, yet the uninterrupted motion sequence
   still reads as a regular row of tall dark openings. Increase the perceptual
   difference using the already supported entry/gallery/patio vocabulary—for
   example clearer entry-side grouping, canopy depth/width, patio threshold, or
   pauses between groups—while keeping all exact placement decisions labeled
   inference. Do not add random pane noise or claim an observed cadence.

## Nonblocking discrepancies and limitations

1. The GenAI brick has faint pause-and-search recurrence and minor boundary
   uncertainty in the source tile. It is subordinate in all rendered evidence
   and is **not** a reason to regenerate or reject this material.
2. The brick is albedo-only with scalar roughness. That is an honest minimal map
   set here; a speculative normal/roughness texture would not solve the current
   whole-facade blockers.
3. The accepted high WSW face is low-information and largely scalar. It limits
   recognition from these two angles, but changing the accepted high is outside
   this repair and is not required before correcting the low blockers above.
4. The ENE view cannot show the whole tower because of site occlusion. Its
   supplemental label is truthful and nonblocking because two measured WSW
   whole views already show both exact parts.
5. Planter proxies are small and visibly schematic at close range. They are
   acceptable supporting cues once the larger rhythm/hierarchy blockers are
   resolved.
