## Decision

**PASS_WITH_LIMITATION for promotion to one future reversible, fail-closed live
integration study.**

This is not denominator credit, a live-world acceptance, or an as-built claim.
It means the standalone candidate crosses the recognizability,
believability, visual-interest, source-boundary, and motion-stability bar needed
to justify fresh exact-current live integration and evidence.

The after state reads as a specific two-storey Site-12 row building without its
address, source ID, map position, or warm-white hue. Two independent,
target-bound cue families carry the result:

1. exact long projecting windowed wings flank the real deep footprint setback,
   with runs `12` and `13` forming a stacked two-level recessed center; and
2. the shallow dark side-gable and broad eaves cap that solid/void cadence as a
   coherent low residential silhouette.

The grouped wing openings, balcony slabs, and rail silhouettes support those
large forms. They do not replace them. The result is economical low-poly game
art rather than realism, but it is no longer a flat generic extruded footprint.

There are **no visual or technical blockers** to the one live study. The dark
recess fields remain somewhat diagrammatic, the rail/access reading is
subordinate at distance, the inferred window cadence is regular, and unknown
faces remain visibly provisional. These are ranked nonblockers below. Two
metadata inconsistencies also need correction in the fresh live evidence, but
they do not change the inspected pixels, exact receiver geometry, or this
standalone art verdict.

## Matched visual judgment

### Baseline

The baseline is an undifferentiated generated wall-and-roof mass. In the whole
view it is effectively another pale strip among neighboring placeholders. In
the closer oblique it exposes the exact zigzag footprint, but its repeated
generic surface and absence of openings, voids, rails, or eave hierarchy give a
player no target-specific architectural sentence.

### Prototype

The prototype makes the frozen footprint legible as architecture:

- the two long outer blocks visibly project toward the player and carry two
  complete rows of grouped paired openings;
- the central footprint setback becomes a deep, two-storey circulation/balcony
  zone instead of another coplanar window strip;
- four dark void fields are separated by real pale slabs and simple rail
  silhouettes, so the center reads as two levels rather than one black decal;
- broad dark eaves produce a stable shadow break over each mapped SSE face; and
- the shallow gable gives the roof a coherent low residential cap rather than
  a flat placeholder lid.

The whole after frame is intentionally not a glamour close-up: target projected
bounds are only about `0.330 x 0.122` of the frame at a `48.51 m` first-hit
distance. Even there, two pale wings, the dark two-level center, and the dark
roof/eave line remain legible. The closer oblique raises the bounds to about
`0.582 x 0.261` at `39.74 m` and exposes the real wing-to-setback depth. The
same hierarchy survives the materially changed sun and grayscale inspection.

The center does not claim a literal target-specific door. At ground level it
reads as a recessed access/breezeway family, which is the strongest statement
the source supports. Adding an invented focal entrance would be less truthful
than the present restrained treatment.

## Requested confusion comparison

- **1439 Chinook (`w95934144`)** has a different observed SSE sentence: one
  strongly recessed balcony stack near one end, a broad quiet upper-window
  field, and a near-blank opposite end. The reviewed 1441 is instead balanced
  around two projecting windowed wings and a paired central two-level recess.
- **1443 Chinook (`w95934143`)** has exact-address evidence for a rear stacked
  patio/balcony component, but no observation maps that component to a receiver
  side. Transferring it onto 1441 would be false; the reviewed candidate does
  not do so.
- **1438 (`w95934131`) and 1440 (`w95934129`) Chinook** have no defensible
  target exterior observations. They therefore cannot donate an opening or
  balcony schedule. This review does not pretend to prove their unseen real
  facades differ; it accepts 1441 on its own target-bound SSE observation and
  exact frozen footprint cadence while leaving those peers uncredited.

Within that truth boundary, 1441 is top-1 as game art. Its symmetry, two
projecting wings, central paired setback faces, two-level dark circulation
zone, and continuous shallow gable/eave composition are a coherent combination,
not a generic Site-12 palette or address token.

The declared review interval moves the stock player continuously along the
public-side tangent while the camera orientation remains fixed. Across the

- wing openings, center slabs, void divisions, and roof/eave edges remain fixed
  to the building without crawl or visible phase reset;
- thin rail silhouettes remain stable when visible; they fade by distance and
  value, not by temporal shimmer;
- the warm-ivory GenAI micro-surface mips into a quiet field. There is no hard
  vertical or horizontal seam, repeated stain, light gradient, or popping
  motif;
- the `3 m` source cell is not pixel-perfect under a deliberately assembled raw
  repeat, but its faint recurrence never becomes a facade-scale grid in the
  rendered evidence; and
- frames `156..158` pass behind a nearby exact world mass. This is a brief
  honest occlusion, not a camera cut or evidence crop; the target reappears
  unchanged and the remaining `118` interval frames are ample for the motion
  judgment.

No texture regeneration is justified by this gate. Geometry carries identity;
the generated albedo is successfully limited to quiet material believability.

## Ranked nonblockers and smallest live checks

1. **Dark-center semantic compression.** At the widest distance the four voids
   can initially resemble a dark glazed grid because pickets merge into shadow.
   Preserve the deep setback and slab divisions. In fresh live motion, adjust
   only rail thickness/value or void depth if the circulation read disappears;
   do not brighten the entire void or invent interiors.
2. **No focal literal doorway.** The ground recess plausibly communicates
   access, but it is not a distinct entrance. Keep this restraint unless a
   target-bound source supports a door; recognizability does not require an
   invented front-door token.
3. **Regular inferred wing cadence.** Three columns by two rows on each wing is
   visibly economical. Keep it disclosed and reversible, never propagate it as
   a cohort default, and change anchors only if better evidence appears.
4. **Subtle gable pitch.** The broad dark eave is stronger than the roof slope
   at normal distance. Verify the exact live wall/roof replacement does not
   flatten or double the silhouette; do not increase rise merely for spectacle.
5. **Protected return finish.** Neutral unknown faces are honest but read as a
   prototype boundary. Carry only physically necessary base/roof-edge/material
   continuity in live art. Add no unsupported windows, rails, entries, or
   damage.
6. **Faint raw stucco recurrence.** Retain the reviewed `3 m` repeat, mipmaps,
   scalar roughness, and no-companion-map boundary for the first live study.
   Revisit only if the actual island lighting makes a seam or repeat obvious.

None blocks the live study.

## Metadata consistency findings

Two exact-current documentation defects should be fixed in the next live
evidence contract. They do not alter this visual verdict or require art
regeneration:

1. The authoritative target packet, standalone README, and requested review set
   name 1439, 1443, 1438, and 1440 Chinook. The capture manifest instead pins
   source IDs `w95934144,w95934107,w95934118,w95934120` (1439 Chinook, 1420
   Striped Bass, 1419 Striped Bass, and 1449 Croaker), and the focused evidence
   test asserts that alternate array. This review used the authoritative packet
   and requested Chinook set. Fresh live metadata must use one reconciled set.
2. The config's `target_stucco.application` text says the stucco also applies to
   eaves, while the factory, rendered evidence, and technical test correctly use
   the dark roof surrogate for `MappedSSEBroadEaves`. Correct the descriptive
   string so it matches the actual material assignment.

A green contract test does not erase these semantic mismatches; it currently
pins them. They are nonblocking for the candidate geometry and art because the
actual run scope, materials, hashes, and pixels are unambiguous. They must not
be copied forward into accepted live provenance.
