## Decision

The bounded high-SE facade language passes the candidate art gate. Across close, sprayed, ordinary-gameplay, oblique, and changed-light views, it preserves the observed hierarchy of warm-light tower panels, narrow taupe accents, dark floor-stacked glazing, a cooler light podium field with distinct opening families, a central curtain-wall strip, and a localized ground/address sequence. The many tower openings remain stable vertical stacks rather than collapsing into sparkle, moire, or an unrelated texture grid.

This is not acceptance of faithful whole-building resemblance. The generated low part is `15 m` / five levels while the locally recorded real podium is seven levels, and the frozen composite simplifies the real tower/podium proportions. The accepted scope is the observed high-SE language on the exact high receiver, the partial high-ENE material continuation, and the conservative decision to leave the low/shared/unobserved schedules unchanged.

## Gate review

- Material hierarchy and albedo: **pass**. Warm-light tower panels, slightly cooler podium, muted taupe accents, neutral supports, charcoal surrounds, and dark blue-grey glazing remain separable without false metal, emission, photographic reflections, or fixed shading. The podium is correctly conservative: the reference permits a homogeneous field until the small-unit bond is proven.
- Module completeness and joins: **pass**. Each opening has all four frame edges. The curtain wall, ground bays, entrance/transom/identifier, louver, and canopy terminate in ordinary field. No jamb, sill, frame, band, address glyph, or opening is created, resized, duplicated, or deleted at a run or part seam.
- Cadence and physical scale: **pass as reference-relative prototype**. Ten narrow tower columns read as floor-stacked vertical glazing separated by broad light fields and subordinate accent strips. Wide, medium, and narrow podium openings remain visibly distinct rather than becoming one universal bay. Bounding boxes stay within the research's coarse ranges; no surveyed/as-built count claim is made.
- Target recognition and address-side legibility: **pass within the current receiver**. At ordinary distance, the twin tower stacks, broad central panel field, podium transition, and dark ground band form a coherent Isle House address elevation. `39`, the entrance, curtain-wall strip, louver, and canopy are clearest in the close and changed-light views and appropriately subordinate at route distance.
- Ordinary-camera readability and repetition: **pass**. The `170` tower modules produce the observed story-stack rhythm, not a material tile. In `03`, `04`, and `05` they retain consistent vertical cadence with no dashed sparkle, diagonal moire, screen-door shimmer, phase reset, or four-corner artifact.
- Exact scope and unobserved-side restraint: **pass as evidenced**. Complete modules are restricted to high-SE runs `5..7`; high-ENE runs `8..9` receive material continuity only. High runs `0..4` and `10..12`, every low-part run, and the hidden high/low interface receive no inferred opening program. The oblique capture visibly leaves the unobserved return generic.
- Spray appearance: **pass**. `02-close-sprayed.png` retains a visible tag on the `39` entrance/glazing area while the manifest records the underlying exact high wall as hit owner. The tag does not expose clipping or a relief gap.
- New defects: **none found**. No frame fragment, doubled module, facade spill onto the low part, invented balcony/garage/rear service program, false emissive glass, deep silhouette break, or unstable fine pattern is visible.

## Residual limitation

The result is a reference-relative unique elevation, not a rectified as-built survey. Exact window counts, accent order, panel joints, small-unit masonry bond, louver pitch, and metre offsets remain unknown; the homogeneous podium field is the documented safe fallback, not a claim about a product or bond. The dominant residual is the prohibited five-level-versus-seven-level podium/massing discrepancy. No facade-only correction is required by this review; faithful whole-building proportion would require a separately authorized receiver/massing decision.
