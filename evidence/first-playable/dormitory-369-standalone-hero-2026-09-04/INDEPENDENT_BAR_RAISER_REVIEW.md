## Decision

**PASS_WITH_LIMITATION for standalone promotion to one reversible live
integration.**

This is not denominator credit and does not accept a live Dormitory 369. It
means the standalone hero has crossed the recognition and believability bar
needed to justify a new exact-receiver wall-and-roof replacement/evidence pass.

The after state is top-1 Dormitory 369 from the ordinary grounded views without
its hue, name, address, source ID, or map position. Two independent
source-supported cue families carry the result:

1. five real occupied tiers with a legible five-row broad/narrow opening rhythm,
   broad-window eyebrows, and a differentiated ground band; and
2. a long light field composed with a shallow darker end/stair volume and thin
   eave as one building-scale hierarchy.

The limitations are nonblocking. The exact `8 x 5` schedule is visibly regular
and remains disclosed production inference; thin eyebrows, mullions, and eave
have not yet received a motion/aliasing test; and the 66 protected runs form an
honest but visibly provisional neutral shell. None removes the two recognition
cues or makes the mapped public face read as procedural wallpaper in the sealed
stills.

## Pixel-first before/after judgment

I opened all six original `1440x900` PNGs at original detail and fixed the
visual judgment before reading the README, manifest, config, source packet,
decoy packets, labels, or contracts.

### Before

All three exact-pose before frames show the prior target as a distant low pale
strip. It has no readable occupied tiers, no end/stair hierarchy, no opening
family, and no believable dormitory scale. The alternate sun does not recover
any identity. This is the same identity-critical failure found in the earlier
P3 live-material review.

### After

The same poses now show a five-storey institutional-residential bar rather than
a `6 m` slab:

- five clearly separated opening rows make the occupied-tier count legible in
  both the complete view and closer WSW oblique;
- broad divided openings alternate with narrow openings, producing a facade
  sentence rather than one repeated square tile;
- shallow dark eyebrows add a second scale to the broad openings without
  turning into a continuous striped overlay;
- the ground row changes role through broad glazing proxies, opaque panels, and
  small base vents, so the five rows are not one vertically stamped module;
- the end/stair volume creates a strong vertical stop, while the thin eave
  closes the long horizontal composition; and
- the unobserved remainder stays quiet and visible rather than being hidden by
  framing or filled with copied modules.

At the complete pose the after entity occupies about `0.094` of frame height
versus `0.039` before, at roughly `152.18 m` LOS. At the closer WSW pose it
occupies `0.127` versus `0.052`, at roughly `121.74 m`. The recognition gain is
therefore a real massing/facade correction at ordinary player distance, not a
close crop or telephoto trick.

The changed-light after frame preserves the five tiers, broad/narrow alternation,
dark end/stair stop, eave, and base distinction. After the blind read, I also
examined temporary grayscale conversions outside the evidence tree. The same
hierarchy remains top-1 by shape, rhythm, projection, and value; color supports
the building but is not required to identify it.

## Keep / residual corrections

### Keep

- Keep the five-tier silhouette, mapped facade length, end/stair stop, thin
  eave, and light/dark value hierarchy. These are the long-distance payload.
- Keep complete broad and narrow openings with real shallow projection/recess,
  and keep the ground row differentiated from the four upper rows.
- Keep every opening/module choice limited to exact runs `0..5`; leave runs
  `6..71` quiet until additional side-specific evidence exists.
- Keep the roof and structural collision at the same inferred landing. Never
  retain a hidden `6 m` shell or collider under the hero.

### Smallest residual fixes, in priority order

1. **Run a short live motion/approach gate before acceptance.** Eyebrows,
   mullions, vents, and the thin eave are near their smallest stable screen size
   at `122–152 m`. Thicken or simplify only the element that actually shimmers,
   crawls, or disappears; do not enlarge all detail for spectacle.
2. **Protect the hierarchy from over-regularity.** The current exact `8 x 5`
   cadence passes as bounded production inference, but do not propagate it to
   protected faces or treat it as surveyed. If a closer rights-cleared source
   later reveals grouping breaks, revise anchors to those observations rather
   than adding random offsets, arbitrary dark panes, or procedural noise.
3. **Unify the protected continuation without inventing it.** In live light,
   tune only the neutral shell's value and physically necessary roof/base
   continuity if it looks like a diagnostic block. Do not add windows, stairs,
   entries, color bands, or copied WSW modules to unknown runs.
4. **Verify the base at a nearer ordinary-player angle.** Preserve its broad
   glazing/panel/vent family, but ensure it remains a grounded base rather than
   five more small tokens. Add no entrance claim unless a source supports one.

These are live-evidence and polish limitations, not reasons to rebuild the
standalone candidate.
