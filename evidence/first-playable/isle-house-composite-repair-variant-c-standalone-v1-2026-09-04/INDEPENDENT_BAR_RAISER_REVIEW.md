## Bar-raiser assessment

### Recognizability — PASS

The accepted folded light tower remains the dominant long-range mass. Variant C
adds a warm handcrafted-looking brick base, two-level live-work scale, private
entry cues, and patio/planter thresholds. Together these provide multiple
independent Isle House cues and materially reduce the low part's Hawkins/490
confusion risk. The low work does not rely on color, a plaque, or a tiny motif as
its sole discriminator; its broad massing and solid/void rhythm remain legible
in whole views and motion.

### Believability and hierarchy — PASS

The object now has a coherent high/mid/low read: tall light folded tower, quiet
podium transition/crown, then darker occupied brick live-work base. Dark glazing
is still opaque and stylized, but frames, half-door splits, canopies, patios,
planters, and differing panel proportions provide enough depth and scale to
avoid a flat texture-card reading. The broad brick pauses keep the facade from
becoming a storefront strip. Changed light produces consistent form shading
without revealing baked illumination.

### Motion, material repetition, and seams — PASS

The complete `65..215` interval is stable while the stock player walks
`19.8834915 m` across the public run `2 -> 3` corner. The brick course remains
continuous across the join; no popping, shimmer, moire, obvious UV discontinuity,
or moving shadow baked into an albedo appears. Faint brick recurrence and small
run-joint variations in the shallow caps are visible only on close inspection
and do not become distracting in motion. Under the owner's progress-over-perfect
tileability rule, they are non-blocking.

### Source and claim boundary — PASS

The source supports warm brick/handcrafted eye-level material, two-level
live-work homes, individual entries/Dutch doors/patios, and a seventh-story
podium as a whole-building fact. It does not map an exact low-run schedule.
Variant C truthfully labels the four arrangements, eight placements, widths,
spacing, sequence, upper band proportions/relief, scalar NNW value, opaque
glazing, canopy/patio depths, and planter proxies as reversible production
inference.

The candidate does **not** claim an upper opening/window/floor/story schedule,
a literal seven-story correction to the generated five-level low part, an NNW
opening or cladding schedule, or a private/rear/courtyard facade. The protected
NNW fallback has zero authored openings. Source photography and source URLs are
absent from the runtime package.

## Ranked non-blocking limitations

1. The `11.45–17.38 m` podium body remains a large low-information scalar plane.
   It is now bounded by convincing transfer/crown/cap hierarchy, and adding an
   opening grid without new mapped evidence would be a worse source violation.
2. The NNW fallback is conspicuously plain in its direct view. That is an honest
   protected-side treatment, not an as-built claim; it is calm and free of the
   failed grid, so it does not block whole-object acceptance.
3. Opaque dark glazing compresses some frame, half-door, canopy, and planter
   detail at the widest view. The four large-form arrangements still remain
   distinct, especially across the complete motion interval.
4. The brick has faint repeat character and the shallow upper caps show minor
   run-joint variation at close range. Neither becomes an obvious seam,
   distracting recurrence, or lighting artifact in motion.
5. The player avatar occludes the central module in any single motion frame,
   but continuous movement plus the two whole views and ENE supplement expose
   the complete reviewed rhythm.
