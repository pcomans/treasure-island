The actual emitted ArrayMesh data passes the changed winding/normal check. All five records are non-indexed; I inspected sequential vertex triples rather than treating empty indices as empty geometry. The sole source emitter explicitly begins `Mesh.PRIMITIVE_TRIANGLES`; the runtime receipt does not independently serialize primitive type. Clockwise fronts were computed as `(a-c) cross (a-b)` and compared with every supplied vertex normal. Barrel: 140 triangles, all upward (minimum front Y 0.9625547), minimum front/normal dot 0.99998498. Crown ends: 280 triangles, front +Z and rear −Z throughout, minimum dot 1. Lip/cap: 280 triangles, outward +Z front and upward curved cap, minimum dot 0.99999993. No degenerate triangles occur. Actual annex top has 57 upward triangles; unchanged walls have 118 nondegenerate triangles. Tiny normal components around 0.00002 are recorded native precision effects, not reversed faces.

The consumed barrel shader explicitly uses `cull_back`; it no longer depends on double-sided rendering to hide inward orientation. Crown/lip and annex shaders retain `cull_disabled`, but their actual front winding and normals are outward independently of that setting. The new flashing uses ordinary BoxMesh with unchanged material-helper behavior; its primitive arrays were not included in the ArrayMesh dump. The lip is an open trim front/cap, not claimed as a closed solid. No closed-ring or manifold claim is needed for that trim.
