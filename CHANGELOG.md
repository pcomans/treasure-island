# Grand Treasure Island: what changed in each build

https://grandtreasureisland.vercel.app/

Every build of the game has a number, the one in its title on the [releases page](https://github.com/pcomans/treasure-island/releases) and in its version on [itch.io](https://phppco.itch.io/grand-treasure-island). Its entry here says what changed for players, newest build first; an entry's number is the build it was written for, and the release that first carries it may have a slightly larger one. For each build after 2084, the release's notes start with its changes, and its itch.io downloads carry them as `CHANGELOG.txt`.

## Build 2341 (2026-11-20)

- You can walk into Building 1, the Administration Building: its middle front doors stand open into the great curved hall, with its wood columns, balcony and tall windows.
- Along the hall's east wall runs a long painted mural of the island's story, told by sea lions, otters, pelicans and other California sea animals; it appears as you come near the doors.
- Cars stop at the doors; only people on foot go in.

## Build 2336 (2026-11-19)

- The doors of the Job Corps buildings, and of the other modern buildings built the same way, no longer have a strip of grass between the doorstep and the door: the step now runs into the doorway, and garage doorways have a floor too.
- At the Job Corps dormitory (Building 369), the step of the stair-tower door now runs to the tower wall, with no strip of grass beside it.
- Plants and hedges no longer grow over the doorsteps of the modern apartment buildings, and the grass beside the steps of the Avenue D gatehouse and the side door of 490 Avenue of the Palms is now paving; the lobby step at 490 Avenue of the Palms no longer sinks half under the sidewalk.
- Doorsteps no longer sink into garden beds, lawns or park walks: the beds' mulch and the walks stop at the step and at the doorway, so the step at 490 Avenue of the Palms that was buried in mulch shows whole, with no bark chips scattered over it.

## Build 2307 (2026-11-18)

- The Job Corps dormitory on California Avenue (Building 369) looks more like itself: pale walls and slate stair towers, sunshades, blinds and white sashes at its windows, an overhanging roof slab, and the antennas and duct on its north tower.
- The Job Corps campus has its chain-link fence along Seven Seas Avenue, its north side, 5th Street and round the 4th Street lot; its gates and the streets into the campus stay open.

## Build 2302 (2026-11-18)

- On the web, the game uses much less memory, so it should stop reloading on iPhones.
- Fences can be seen from slightly farther away.

## Build 2270 (2026-11-18)

- On the web, the game no longer freezes for a moment the first time something comes into view: its graphics are prepared during loading, which takes several seconds longer.

## Build 2221 (2026-11-17)

- Chester no longer blocks his own flashlight when he starts running at night.
- Two log seats that stood on the Cityside Park promenade and stopped cars now sit in the planting beside it.

## Build 2198 (2026-11-17)

- The windows on the big box truck, the motorhome and the shuttle no longer flicker.
- Planted beds, the promenade and the street planting strips now have wood-chip mulch instead of flat brown, with loose chips up close on desktop.
- The changelog in the downloads and the release notes starts with the game's website.

## Build 2176 (2026-11-16)

- Avenue of the Palms beside the Isle House park is level now; the hill under the road is gone.
- The park's north-west corner is level and planted, without the grass bank beside the avenue or the berm by the cycle track.

## Build 2144 (2026-11-16)

- On the web, the game's data is kept on your device after the first visit, so later launches skip the big download until a new build is out. Not yet on iPhone and iPad.
- On the web, the loading line says whether the game is downloading or loading from your device.
- Web: less memory churn from sound while you play.

## Build 2117 (2026-11-16)

- The release notes on GitHub and the itch.io downloads now say what changed in each build.
- The pause menu shows the build number.
- On an iPhone or iPad, the start screen says how to add the game to the home screen for full screen, and that silent mode must be off to hear sound.
- Added to a phone's home screen, the game opens full screen, like an app.
- On an Android phone or tablet, tapping to play also makes the game full screen.
- The Mac app has been signed and notarized by Apple since build 2084.

## Build 2084 (2026-11-16)

- The park outside Isle House is in: lawn, chip beds, young pines, timber benches, chairs and logs, with a grass bank beside Avenue of the Palms.
- In Options, the time-of-day slider marks sunrise and sunset and says "Night is experimental".
- Web: reflections take about 250 MB less graphics memory, which helps on phones.

## Build 2047 (2026-11-16)

- Pelicans, cormorants and gulls rest on the breakwater.

## Build 2027 (2026-11-16)

- A Windows build (64-bit) joins the Mac and Linux builds. It loads the island on a test machine but has not yet been played on a real Windows PC.
- The downloads are named after the game and the build number.

## Build 2010 (2026-11-16)

- The game is now called Grand Treasure Island; options chosen in earlier builds carry over.
- The glare of a low sun on glass is back.
- The loading screen names each step as it runs.
- Play starts at 12:30 on the island's date, whatever the real time.

## Build 1962 (2026-11-16)

- No changes to the game.

## Build 1933 (2026-11-16)

- No changes to the game.
