# Building 1 hall mural provenance

- **`mural_1.png` to `mural_4.png`**: the mural band on the curved east wall of Building 1's
  main hall, in order from the north end (`mural_1`) to the south end (`mural_4`). Each
  tile is one quarter of the band; they join edge to edge and nothing repeats.
  - This is the game's own painting, not a reconstruction. Owner's idea (2026-11-19):
    redraw the hall's mural with marine animals in place of every person. Its layout of
    scenes follows the hall's 1975 Bicentennial mural by Lowell Nesbitt (artist and year
    from the Treasure Island Museum's self-guided tour flyer), credited under Murals in
    `game/resources/ui/credits.json`. No figure, face, lettering, emblem or brushwork of
    the original is copied: the band keeps only its left-to-right sequence (open sea and
    a tall ship, islands, a harbour welcome, an old steamer harbour, a battle here
    replaced by a squall and a rescue, a dusk landing, a golden evening at sea, a
    splashdown, a diver and an undersea future) and its colour journey from blue through
    orange to turquoise.
  - Every animal is a species native to California's coast and waters (owner,
    2026-11-19): California sea lion, Pacific harbor seal, northern elephant seal,
    southern sea otter, common bottlenose and short-beaked common dolphins, humpback
    whale, brown pelican, western gull, Brandt's and double-crested cormorants, giant
    Pacific octopus, Dungeness crab, California spiny lobster, blue rockfish, garibaldi,
    northern anchovy, leopard shark, bat ray, Pacific sea nettle; the ribbon carries
    Pacific sand dollars and ochre sea stars. No people, text, real flags or insignia,
    weapons or fighting.
  - Made in this project on 2026-11-19 with `tools/generate-texture` (Codex image
    generation) from text prompts written from a neutral description of the 2023 hall
    photographs' layout; no photograph, artist name or title was given to the
    generator. Each quarter was generated whole at 2084 x 756 (style chosen by the owner
    from three tests: soft painted acrylic); quarters 2 and 4 were given the previous
    generated quarter as a continuity reference. Selection: 16 generated quarters, 4 used.
  - Composed with ImageMagick: each quarter cut just right of its seam object (the
    harbour signal mast, a palm trunk, a lattice watch mast) and scaled to a quarter of
    an 8340 x 756 band; the first and last 4 % are calm sea and sky, so the band can be
    trimmed or stretched to the wall; a faint letter-like smudge on the steamer's hull in
    quarter 2 was blurred out; the ribbon (8340 x 78, about 9 % of the height) was drawn
    in code: a painted-looking gradient through navy #1c2b4f (0.00), #1e3f78 (0.08), blue #2468ad (0.15), cyan #2b93bd (0.21), teal #36ad9a (0.27), green #5fb553 (0.31), #b9c53d (0.35), yellow #e3b234 (0.38), orange #e5852c (0.42), #d4532c (0.46), red #a8352f (0.50), maroon #74283c (0.54), plum #5c2440 (0.62), #4f223f (0.70), #3a2140 (0.745), near-black navy #1b1d31 (0.78), blue #1f3f7a (0.82), #1f6f8f (0.86), teal #24907a (0.89), green #7db342 (0.93), yellow #d2c44c (0.965), pale cream #d8d4c2 (1.00), cream edge lines,
    alternating cream sand dollars and ochre sea stars, a close row of seven sea stars at
    0.858-0.92 and plain from 0.925. The 8340 x 834 master (10:1) was resampled
    (Lanczos) to 5120 x 512, cut into four 1280 x 512 quarters and each squeezed to
    1024 x 512, so the shapes are true on a band about 10 times as long as it is tall.
  - Masters, prompt logs and the work scripts are kept outside Git with the study.
