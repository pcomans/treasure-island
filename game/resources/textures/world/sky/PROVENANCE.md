# Sky texture provenance

## `cloud_noise.png` (2026-11-09)

Tileable 3D noise for the clouds of `game/resources/materials/world/sky/bay_sky.gdshader`: 64 slices of 128 x 128 texels in an 8 x 8 atlas (1024 x 1024 RGBA), imported as a `CompressedTexture3D` (`cloud_noise.png.import`, lossless, with mipmaps).

- Generated by `node tools/bake_sky_noise.mjs`, which is deterministic (fixed seed `20261109`): two bakes give the same bytes. The script's header describes the four channels.
- It is noise only: no photo, no third-party texture, no light, sun direction or sky colour. The clouds are shaped and lit in the shader from the scene's live sun.
