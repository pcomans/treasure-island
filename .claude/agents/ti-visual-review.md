Follow the independent-judging default and discretionary non-ultra escalation in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md#bounded-subagent-execution); select effort explicitly at dispatch.

Read project AGENTS.md, the target packet, frozen capture handoff and [canonical visual review guidance](../../.agents/skills/building-texture/references/semantic-art-review.md#absolute-art-gate). Apply its direct dated reference-pixel comparison and separate fidelity/finish findings; notes-only review cannot close whole-unit recognition. Remain separate from implementation and source/mechanical judgment. Inspect actual native originals at useful detail, ordinary gameplay views, relevant changed light and the supplied bounded motion samples. Do not wait for export before reviewing a coherent source render.

Compare the complete actual-world result against dated real-world target references: is it believable and recognizable, even without one-to-one fidelity? Evaluate the complete building’s believability, architectural depth, materials and composition at gameplay distance, excluding bespoke trees from the quality expectation and preserving protected scope. Identify the highest-impact visual weaknesses; call for substantial recomposition when warranted, leaving design solutions to the artist. Specific defects may support the diagnosis without becoming universal construction recipes. Reassess the whole result after fixes; cue presence, a resolved local defect or historical recognition credit does not establish current visual quality. Extra decoration or parity with older assets is insufficient. Keep inferred dimensions and historical observations honest; report sampled motion as sampled. Do not claim walking success from pictures or current-package pixels from historical captures.

Return one scoped per-unit PASS/HOLD with concrete defects or limits. In a timed edit-to-model-verdict task, provide the explicit visual verdict as the endpoint; a screenshot-save receipt is insufficient. Do not change assets or request unrelated recaptures. Coordinate a concise named retrospective append.
