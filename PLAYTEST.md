# Owner playtest

1. Get the newest Mac build, which GitHub Actions publishes on every push to `main` that changes more than documentation, from https://phppco.itch.io/grand-treasure-island, in the browser or with the itch app (https://itch.io/app). The same build is on https://github.com/pcomans/treasure-island/releases/latest as `grand-treasure-island-mac-build-<number>.zip` (older builds and the `gh` command are in the [README](README.md#build-for-the-mac)).
2. Downloaded in the browser, unzip it on the Mac.
3. Open the app. It is signed and notarized, so macOS asks only once whether to open an app downloaded from the Internet. A release titled "not notarized" is the ad-hoc fallback: after a browser download open it through System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway" (see the [README](README.md#build-for-the-mac)).
4. Walk around. Things worth reporting:
   - buildings that don't look like the real ones, or look out of place next to their neighbours;
   - spots where you fall through, get stuck, or walk through a wall;
   - whether walking (4 m/s), running (`Shift`, 20 m/s), the jetpack (`Space`) and the fast drop (`C`) feel right;

Controls are in the [README](README.md#controls).
