# Owner playtest

1. Ask an agent for a Mac build, or run `tools/build-mac.sh` in the devcontainer.
2. Copy `build/mac/treasure-island-mac-<commit>.zip` to the Mac and unzip it.
3. Right-click the app → Open (first time only; it is not notarized).
4. Walk around. Things worth reporting:
   - buildings that don't look like the real ones, or look out of place next to their neighbours;
   - spots where you fall through, get stuck, or walk through a wall;
   - whether walking (4 m/s), running (`Shift`, 20 m/s) and the jetpack (`Space`) feel right;
   - whether spraying (click) lands where you aim.

Controls are in the [README](README.md#controls). The earlier, release-bound
version of this guide is in [PLAYTEST_HISTORY_2026-10-03.md](PLAYTEST_HISTORY_2026-10-03.md).
